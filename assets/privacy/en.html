Privacy policy

Version of 28 October 2026. Applies to the website, the web app and the “Old Maps” mobile apps.

1. Who processes the data

The controller is Aliaksandr Pyrkin, a self-employed professional income tax payer, Republic of Belarus, taxpayer number AC2556232. Contact for data questions: alnpyrkin@gmail.com.

2. What we store on the server

  • Account: e-mail address and password (encrypted; we never see the password itself), sign-up date, date and version of your consent to this policy. If you sign in with Google or Apple — the e-mail address and name from that account (your Google or Apple password is never sent to us; Apple may give a hidden relay address instead of your e-mail).
  • Access: trial period and paid access period.
  • Marks in your profile: marks, areas, tracks, arrows, labels and projects — so that they are the same on your phone and computer. Deleted marks are erased from the server after 30 days.
  • Sent marks: when you send a mark to another user by their e-mail, the mark, your e-mail and your message are kept until the recipient responds.
  • Reports of inaccuracies on the map and in the map catalogue that you sent, and the replies to them.
  • Trial protection: irreversible fingerprints (SHA-256 hash) of your e-mail address and device ID — so that the free trial is given only once. Neither the e-mail nor the device ID can be recovered from a fingerprint.
  • Shared links: the mark you sent (coordinates, category, caption) and up to three photos. After 30 days the link stops working and the app deletes it from the server together with the photos.
  • AI request counter (“What is it?”, “What is this symbol?”, automatic map georeferencing) — how many requests you made per day, for the daily limit.
  • Technical server logs (IP address, request time) — to prevent abuse.

3. What stays only on your device

Maps you uploaded, offline maps, photos of finds and settings are stored in the memory of your phone or browser and are not sent to our server. Your location is determined on the device and is not stored anywhere (except marks and tracks you saved yourself).

4. Purposes and legal basis

  • Account, trial and payment — performance of the contract (terms of use) with you.
  • Storing data on servers outside your country and sending photos to recognition services — your consent given at sign-up.
  • Preventing abuse, daily limits and preventing the free trial from being obtained again — our legitimate interest in keeping the service running.

We do not sell data, do not show ads and do not use data for profiling.

5. Where the data is stored

The database is hosted by the cloud service Supabase (Supabase Inc.) on servers outside the Republic of Belarus; we plan to move it to the Republic of Belarus (hoster.by) and will update this policy then. Map files are hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub Inc., USA); they contain no personal data. If you are in the European Union or another country, this is a transfer of data outside it; you consent to it at sign-up.

6. Who receives the data

  • The hosting provider of the database server — stores the data on our behalf.
  • Google and Apple — if you sign in with them: they confirm the sign-in and give us your e-mail address and name.
  • Google (Gemini, Google Lens) and OpenRouter — only when you tap “What is it?”, “Similar in Google”, “What is this symbol?” or “Find points by place names”: a photo or a piece of the map is sent to them. A photo for Google Lens is kept by us behind a temporary 15-minute link and then deleted.
  • Map and basemap providers (OpenStreetMap and its services, OpenFreeMap, Esri, MapTiler, EOX, Amazon Web Services for Sentinel-2 imagery, Valhalla for routes, Nominatim for search) — when the map loads they see your IP address and the map area, like any website with a map. For a route they receive the start and end points.
  • The payment provider (Boosty, Google Play, App Store, bePaid) — processes the payment itself; your card details never reach us.
  • Public authorities — only where the law expressly requires it.

7. How long we keep data

Account data and marks in your profile — as long as the account exists. Shared links — 30 days, after which the app deletes them at the next start. Sent marks — until the recipient responds. Technical logs — no longer than 90 days. After you delete your account, the data is deleted from the server immediately and from backups within 30 days. Fingerprints for trial protection are kept for 3 years, including after the account is deleted.

8. Your rights

  • Find out what data we hold about you, correct it, get a copy of it.
  • Delete your account at any time: “Settings” → “Account” → “Delete account”. All your data on the server is deleted with it, except the fingerprints for trial protection (section 2).
  • Withdraw consent — by deleting your account or writing to us; the service cannot work without consent.
  • Lodge a complaint: in Belarus — with the National Personal Data Protection Centre; in the EU — with the data protection authority of your country.

Send requests to alnpyrkin@gmail.com; we answer within 15 days.

9. Browser storage

We do not use advertising or analytics cookies. The app stores in the browser only what it needs to work: sign-in, settings, your marks and maps.

10. Children

The service is not intended for children under 16 without parental consent.

11. Changes

When this policy changes we update the version date and, if the changes are significant, ask for your consent again.
